# Outreach Templates

Channel-specific templates for SaaS launch and growth outreach.

---

## LinkedIn Post — Founder Launch Announcement

**When to use:** Launch day or major milestone announcement from founder account.

**Structure:**
1. Hook — one line on the problem or moment
2. What you built — brief, specific
3. Why it matters — customer proof or concrete result
4. CTA — try it, feedback, or share

**Template:**

```
[Number] years ago I kept running into [problem].

Every tool either [limitation A] or [limitation B].

So we built [product name] — [one line positioning].

[2-3 sentences on what it does, grounded in how it actually works, not features].

Early users have seen [specific result or quote if available].

Link in comments. Happy to answer questions.
```

**Tips:**
- Write from "I" not "we" if founder voice
- Avoid: "revolutionary", "game-changing", "best-in-class"
- Include a screenshot or short demo video
- engagement > reach; write for your niche first

## X (Twitter) Launch Thread

**When to use:** Launch day thread from company or founder account.

**Structure:**
- Tweet 1: Hook — the problem in one sentence
- Tweet 2-n: What it is, how it works, proof
- Final tweet: CTA + payment/try link

**Template:**

```
🧵 A short story about why we built [product name]...

[Problem statement — something specific, not generic]

Most tools solve [symptom]. We went after [root cause].

Here's what that looks like in practice:

[Screenshot / GIF of core feature doing the job]

The stack: [brief mention of how it works if interesting technically]

Result for users: [concrete outcome, ideally with a number]

[Product name] is live today.

[Stripe payment link or free trial link]
```

**Tips:**
- One idea per tweet
- Mix text and visuals
- No thread longer than 8-10 tweets
- End with a clear, single CTA

## Cold Email — Introduction

**When to use:** Reaching prospects who fit the ICP and have not heard of you.

**Structure:**
1. Subject line — specific, personal, no hype
2. Opening — reference (mutual, content they made, problem they have)
3. Body — what you built, who it's for, one proof point
4. CTA — simple next step

**Template:**

```
Subject: [Specific observation about their work or company]

Hi [Name],

I noticed [specific thing about their company/content/product — proves you did homework].

We built [product name] for [specific use case or problem]. It helps [who] do [what outcome] without [the pain point].

[One concrete example or result — "users cut [X] by [Y]" or "we heard from [type of user] that..."]

Happy to show you a quick demo if you're dealing with [problem] right now.

[Time estimate] of your calendar, no slides.

[Your name]
```

**Tips:**
- Research the person before sending
- Personalize the opening — never "I came across your profile"
- Keep it under 150 words total
- No attachments in first email

## Cold Email — Follow-Up Sequence

**When to use:** If no response to initial email. Send 2 follow-ups max.

**Follow-up 1 (3-5 days later):**

```
Subject: Re: [original subject] — one more thing

Hi [Name],

Just circling back. [One sentence add-on — a new data point, a question, or a different angle].

Still happy to show you [product name] — takes 20 minutes.

[Your name]
```

**Follow-up 2 (5-7 days after that):**

```
Subject: [Name] — last one from me

Hi [Name],

Fair if now's not the time. I'll leave you alone.

If [problem they have] comes up later, [product name] is at [link].

Best,
[Your name]
```

## Podcast Outreach

**When to use:** Reaching out to podcasters who cover your niche.

**Target profile:**
- Podcast covers [your niche or industry]
- Audience matches your ICP
- Episode length and format fits your story
- They accept guest pitches (check show notes or website)

**Search terms:**
- `[niche] podcast` / `[niche] interview podcast`
- `site:pitchamonkey.com [niche]` (guest matching)
- `[industry] founders interview`
- `[problem space] podcast`

**Template:**

```
Subject: [Guest pitch — specific episode idea]

Hi [Host name],

I've been listening to [podcast name] for a while — particularly enjoyed the episode on [specific topic]. 

I run [company/product] and wanted to propose a guest spot. We'd be a good fit if you're covering [episode angle that matches your niche].

Our angle: [1-2 sentences on the topic, framed as a conversation not a pitch].

Some topics we could cover:
- [Topic 1 — problem-focused, not product-focused]
- [Topic 2 — relevant to your audience]

[data point or result that would interest your listeners].

Happy to work around your format.

[Your name]
[Link to past appearances or media kit if available]
```

## Partner / Integration Outreach

**When to use:** Reaching complementary SaaS products or platforms for cross-promotion or integration.

**Template:**

```
Subject: Integration idea for [their product] + [your product]

Hi [Name],

I've been using [their product] for [how long] and it's become part of our stack.

We built [your product] to handle [specific adjacent problem]. Right now users have to [manual step or workaround].

A [native integration / Zapier connection / webhook setup] between [their product] and [your product] would save [specific workflow] — something I know your users have asked about.

Would you be open to a quick call to explore whether this is worth building together?

[Your name]
```

---

## General Tips

- Always personalize. Generic outreach gets ignored.
- Lead with the reader's problem, not your product.
- Specific > impressive. Numbers, names, details.
- CTA should always be a next step, not "let me know if interested."
- Keep emails short. If you need 5 paragraphs, you're writing a blog post.
- Track opens/clicks if your email tool supports it.
