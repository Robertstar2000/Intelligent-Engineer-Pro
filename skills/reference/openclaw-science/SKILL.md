# Science SaaS — Product Skill

## Overview

Science is the first SaaS product in MIFECO's three-product stack (Science → Engineering → Project Management). It provides AI-powered research synthesis, scientific analysis, and data reasoning.

**Positioning:** Organizations that need rigorous analysis without large R&D staff — space/aerospace, policy shops, technical startups.

**Launch order:** Science launches FIRST (per board Decision 6). Engineering second, Project Management third.

---

## Core Functions

### Research Synthesis
- Ingest and normalize research data from multiple sources
- Apply AI reasoning to synthesize findings across sources
- Generate structured analysis reports from raw data inputs

### Scientific Analysis
- Pattern recognition in technical datasets
- Hypothesis generation and testing frameworks
- Evidence scoring and confidence weighting

### Data Reasoning
- Multi-source data reconciliation
- Gap identification in existing research
- Forward inference from observed data

---

## Product Specifications

### Target Users
- Technical researchers without large teams
- Space/aerospace organizations
- Policy analysts needing rapid synthesis
- Technical startups needing rigorous analysis

### Integration Points
- Science output → feeds into Engineering (research feeds development)
- Engineering output → feeds into Project Management (tasks are planned)
- All three share a common data layer

### Pricing
- Sold separately (per Decision 10: no bundle)
- Entry tier, professional tier, enterprise tier
- Annual and monthly options
