# Book Barbarian Promotion Checklist

Book Barbarian is a promotion site for science fiction and fantasy books. It is not a publishing platform.

## Fit

- Book is science fiction or fantasy.
- Best fit is standalone or book 1 in a series.
- Book 2 or later alone is usually a weak fit unless using a specific multi-book promotion option.

## Quality

- Professional cover.
- Professional editing.
- Clean formatting.
- Strong blurb.
- Clear genre signal.

## Review Gate

- Aim for at least 10 Amazon.com USA reviews.
- Average rating should be at least 3.5.
- For new releases, prior author review history may help.

## Deal Gate

- Book is free or discounted by at least 50 percent.
- Reader-preferred price is usually free, 0.99, 1.99, or 2.99.
- Promo price must be live on the promotion day.
- If submitting Free Book of the Day, the book must be free on that date.

## Retail Links

Prepare:

- Amazon.com URL
- eBook ASIN
- Kobo URL if available
- Barnes and Noble URL if available
- Apple Books URL if available
- Smashwords or Draft2Digital URL if available

## Submission Data

- name
- email
- role
- book title
- author name
- Amazon.com link
- eBook ASIN
- category: fantasy or science fiction
- subgenre
- promo date
- promo price
- cover
- blurb
- review count and rating

## Decision

Submit only when genre, quality, review, ASIN, and deal-price gates pass.
