## Validation Tools
- **epubcheck** (W3C): https://github.com/w3c/epubcheck
- **Kindle Previewer 3**: https://www.amazon.com/Kindle-Previewer/b?ie=UTF8&node=21381691011

## Notes
- KDP now recommends **EPUB 3** as the preferred format for Kindle eBooks (as of 2025+).
- MOBI is deprecated in favour of EPUB.
- Always validate with BOTH epubcheck AND Kindle Previewer 3 before KDP upload.
- KDP will reject EPUBs with validation errors — fix all errors AND warnings.
