## 1. AI Project Management Market

### Structural Shift — May 2026
Every major PM tool has pivoted to **agentic AI** (AI that initiates/executes, not just responds). This is not incremental — it's a structural category shift.

### Key Competitor Moves (May 5-15, 2026)

| Competitor | Move | Date | Significance |
|------------|------|------|-------------|
| **ClickUp** | Brain 2.0 with agentic multi-step execution | May 12 | Can generate slide decks, dashboards, websites from single prompt. Acquired Qatalog (enterprise search) + Codegen (AI coding). $300M+ ARR. 11M+ agents. Brain Max mobile app. |
| **Coworked** | $1.8M seed for Harmony AI PM | May 12 | "Headless" AI PM coworker — operates inside existing tools (email, Slack, Jira, MS Project). SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001. |
| **Atlassian** | Rovo GA + Jira Agents GA | May 11 | Rovo used by 75%+ Fortune 500. Teamwork Graph: 150B+ connections. Rovo Studio (no-code agent builder) GA. |
| **monday.com** | Relaunch as "AI Work Platform" | May 6 | Native AI agents across entire platform. One-click connectors to Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot, Gemini. Biggest strategic shift since 2021 IPO. |
| **Linear** | Agent launched + MS Teams | Mar-May 2026 | Multi-level sub-teams, web forms, MS Teams integration, custom coding tools. Agent available in-app/Slack/Teams. |

### MS Project Online Retirement
- **Sept 30, 2026** — 4.5 months remaining
- No new tenants since Apr 1, 2026
- Microsoft pushing Planner Premium (Project Manager agent in preview) but **Planner lacks portfolio rollups, executive dashboards, budget tracking**
- Migration pathways: (1) Planner + Power Platform, (2) Project Server Subscription Edition
- **This is MIFECO's window** — enterprises need migration targets now

### Market Metrics
- $5.2B projected by 2027 (17.3% CAGR)
- 78% of PM tools have AI features
- Only ~25% have agentic AI — frontier opportunity
- Pricing band: $7-12/user for "serious tool" category
- Gartner: 33% of enterprise software will include agentic AI by 2028 (from <1% in 2024)

### Unfilled Feature Gaps (MIFECO Opportunity)
1. **Autonomous schedule rescheduling** — full loop: risk detection → revised schedule → stakeholder communication
2. **Stakeholder-intelligent communication** — audience-intelligence layer for different stakeholder needs
3. **Cross-portfolio resource optimization** — multi-scenario modeling with downstream impact projections

## 2. Book Publishing Market

### Industry Legal/Landscape
| Event | Date | Impact |
|-------|------|--------|
| **Meta sued by 6 publishers** (Macmillan, McGraw Hill, Elsevier, Hachette, Cengage + Scott Turow) | May 5 | Alleged Llama trained on copyrighted books from pirate sites. Meta defense: "fair use." Follows Anthropic $1.5B settlement (Sep 2025). |
| **Authors Guild AI Best Practices updated** | May 12 | "AI-generated text is not copyrightable." Undisclosed AI use may breach book contracts. |
| **BISG/BookNet Canada survey** | Apr/May 2026 | 45.8% of publishing professionals use AI. 48% of organizations use AI. Sentiment overwhelmingly negative despite adoption. |

### Self-Publishing & AI Fiction Trends
- **4M+ titles in 2025** (up 32.5%) — self-published rose 38.7% to 3.5M+
- **~45% of indie authors** use generative AI in some way
- **AI audiobook narration**: $8B market, 25% annual growth. Costs $50-200 vs $1,500-3,500 professional
- **KDP Virtual Voice**: Still invite-only beta. 80+ voices, 7 languages, 40% royalty. Amazon-only distribution.
- **Audible policy bifurcated**: ACX = human only; KDP Virtual Voice = AI, Amazon only; third-party aggregators (INaudio/Spotify-Findaway) = AI accepted at lower royalty
