# Market Intelligence Report — June 21, 2026

## 1. AI PROJECT MANAGEMENT TOOLS — 2026 TRENDS

### ClickUp
- **ClickUp Brain2**: Most comprehensive AI layer. "Super Agents," multi-model routing, MCP integration.
- **Pricing:** $5/user/month add-on (min ~$12/user/month with Unlimited plan).
- **2026 highlight:** Brain2 Super Agents work autonomously with full agentic capabilities.

### Asana
- **Asana Intelligence + AI Teammates:** Included in Business+ plan ($24.99/user/month).
- **Strengths:** Best-in-class goal-aligned tracking, workload balancing, smart scheduling.
- **Weaknesses:** Smallest AI surface area; most expensive for AI features.

### Monday.com
- **AI Sidekick + Vibe + Agents:** $8/user/month add-on. Credit-based pricing.
- **Strengths:** Best visual dashboards, strongest enterprise compliance, MCP support (March 2026).

### Notion AI
- **Notion Agents 3.0+:** Autonomous agents with 20-min execution. Access to frontier models.
- **Pricing:** $8/user/month add-on; min ~$16/user/month with Plus plan.
- **Weaknesses:** Weak at task-specific PM features.

### Market Summary
- All four platforms have mature AI by mid-2026. Differentiator is which AI capabilities match the team's biggest time sink.
- **Agentic features** (autonomous multi-step execution) are the hottest new frontier.

## 2. AI SAAS PRICING TRENDS — 2026

- **Hybrid pricing** (base + usage overage) rose from 27% to 41% of SaaS companies in 12 months.
- Pure per-seat fell from 21% to 15%.
- **GitHub Copilot** moved to usage-based billing (June 1, 2026) — flat subscriptions and agentic AI are structurally incompatible.
- Global SaaS market: $375–465 billion (2026).
- AI add-ons add 30–110% to base costs.
- **Recommendation for MIFECO:** Adopt hybrid pricing as industry standard.

## 5. AI BOOK PUBLISHING — 2026 TRENDS
