## 3. AI Consulting Market (June 2026)

### Market Size
- **$14.07B in 2026**, projected **$116.63B by 2035** (26.49% CAGR)
- Broader AI services: $22.3B (2026) → $30B+ by 2028
- Corporate AI investment: $252.3B (2024)
- BCG: companies doubling AI spend in 2026 (0.8% → 1.7% of revenues)

### The "Boutique Inflection" (Key Dynamic)
- **>80% of enterprise AI projects fail** to deliver promised value (RAND)
- **Only 6%** qualify as "AI high performers" (>5% EBIT from AI)
- **Mid-market ($100M-$5B) migrating** from Big Four/MBB to engineering-first boutiques
- Drivers: cost, speed, outcome accountability. Boutiques deliver production systems on fixed fees in 90 days
- Prediction: **40%+ of mid-market spend to boutiques by end of 2027** (up from 25-30%)

### Pricing Models
| Tier | Hourly Rate | Typical Project | Retainer |
|------|-------------|-----------------|----------|
| MBB | $500-$1,000/hr | $500K-$5M+ | $50K-$150K/mo |
| Big 4 | $300-$600/hr | $200K-$2M+ | $50K-$100K/mo |
| Mid-tier | $250-$500/hr | $100K-$1M | $25K-$75K/mo |
| Boutique AI | $150-$350/hr | $50K-$300K | $15K-$50K/mo |
| Independent | $150-$300/hr | $10K-$75K | $5K-$15K/mo |

### Services in Highest Demand
1. Implementation & Deployment (25%+, $3.5B+) — LARGEST segment
2. Digital Transformation Strategy (~35%)
3. Automation Consulting (~30%)
4. Managed Services (fastest-growing, 31.6% CAGR)
5. AI Governance & Compliance (surging — EU AI Act)

### New Platforms (Marketplaces)
| Platform | Model | Fee | Differentiator |
|----------|-------|-----|----------------|
| Zinn Hub | Verified marketplace | Escrow-protected | ID & skill verification |
| InLancingAI | AI-first freelance | 8% flat fee | AI builds briefs/matches |
| Hivve | Zero-fee platform | 0% | AI experts only |
| Consultiverse | Zero-commission | Flat monthly fee | 100% of day rate |

### Key Insights for MIFECO
1. **Implementation & Deployment is largest + most underserved** — focus here
2. **Boutique inflection = MIFECO's opportunity** — $150-$300/hr, fixed-fee <$300K
3. **Managed Services (31.6% CAGR) = recurring revenue** — ongoing AI ops/compliance
4. **AI Governance/EU AI Act = hot emerging vertical** — few firms have real expertise
5. **ROI gap is marketing angle** — position as firm that delivers production systems, not slide decks
6. **Outcome-based pricing** as competitive differentiator — base fee + 10-40% upside
7. **Standard engagement**: scoping ($3.5K) → fixed build ($15K-$40K) → retainer ($6K-$10K/mo)
