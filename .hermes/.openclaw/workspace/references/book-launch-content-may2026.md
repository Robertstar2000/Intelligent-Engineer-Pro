## BOOK 1: BUILT FROM DUST

**The first crew landed on Mars with 47 souls, a mandate to survive, and a world that wanted them dead.**

In **Built from Dust**, the opening volume of the No Blue Sky series, Commander Adaeze Okonkwo leads a crew of engineers and scientists tasked with building humanity's first permanent Mars settlement using nothing but what they brought and what they can pull from the regolith beneath their feet.

Every system failure is plausible. Every engineering solution is grounded in real physics. The atmospheric processors are based on actual ISRU research. The habitat construction mirrors NASA's regolith-3D-printing prototypes. The psychological toll of confinement and distance draws from real Antarctic isolation studies.

But this is more than a technical manual dressed as fiction. It's about leadership under impossible pressure, the bonds that form when survival depends on trust, and the moment settlers stop being visitors and start being Martians.

**Human-authored. Scientifically rigorous. Completely gripping.** All five No Blue Sky books are available now.

## BOOK 2: THE OXYGEN GAMBLE

**They came to Mars to breathe free. Instead, they learned that every breath is a negotiation with physics.**

Three years after landing, Jezero Crater holds 300 souls. Mars feels like home — until the oxygen recyclers begin to fail.

In **The Oxygen Gamble**, a cascading systems failure threatens to suffocate the colony. Chief Engineer Tomás Reyes races a 72-hour atmospheric clock to repair life support while Commander Okonkwo battles Earth agencies that would rather the colony fail.

The technical detail is extraordinary: MOXIE-style oxygen extraction, pressurized habitat thermodynamics, emergency EVA repairs in 38% gravity. But the human drama is what keeps you reading. Who gets backup oxygen when there isn't enough? What happens when the people meant to save you have their own agendas?

**The Oxygen Gamble** understands the most dangerous thing on Mars isn't the environment — it's us. All five books available now.

## BOOK 3: RIVERS UNDER MARS

**Beneath the dead surface of Mars, they found something that changed everything: water.**

The discovery of a massive subsurface aquifer beneath Jezero should be the best news in Mars colonization history. Instead, it ignites the colony's most dangerous conflict.

In **Rivers Under Mars**, the water attracts Earth's most powerful corporations and governments, all claiming rights to the most valuable resource in the solar system. Commander Okonkwo navigates corporate extraction rights, UN mandates, and the growing settler demand for resource sovereignty.

Geologist Dr. Yuki Tanaka maps the aquifer and discovers it's far larger and more geologically active than predicted. Active hydrology means active geology. The scientific understanding of Mars needs rewriting.

**Rivers Under Mars** is the series' turning point — when the colony becomes a society with its own identity and claim to sovereignty. The science is impeccable. The question at its heart — who owns a planet? — has never been more relevant.

All five books available now.

## BOOK 4: THE RED CHARTER

**They didn't just want to survive on Mars. They wanted to govern themselves. That meant writing a constitution for an entire planet.**

The colony has grown to 5,000. They have water, food, oxygen, and industry. What they don't have is self-governance — every law still flows from Earth.

In **The Red Charter**, the settlers draft a constitution for an independent Martian nation. Commander Okonkwo must unite corporate employees, scientists, second-generation Martians who've never seen Earth, and radicals demanding immediate independence.

How do you write a governing document for a society in pressurized habitats? What rights do you guarantee when the environment is lethal? How do you assert sovereignty over a planet Earth considers its property?

**The Red Charter** is the series' most ambitious book — political philosophy, constitutional law, and the birth of a new civilization, grounded in the practical realities of life on Mars.

All five books are complete and ready to read.

## SERIES DESCRIPTION: LUNAR FOUNDATION (4-Book Arc)

**Before Mars, there was the Moon. And the Moon was where humanity learned — the hard way — that space doesn't forgive mistakes.**

The **Lunar Foundation** series is the prequel arc to the No Blue Sky saga, chronicling the decades-long struggle to establish humanity's first permanent off-world settlement. Set fifty years before the Mars landing, these four novels tell the story of the engineers and visionaries who built the infrastructure that made interplanetary civilization possible.

The series opens with Artemis Base, the first lunar settlement, funded by an uneasy coalition of national space agencies and private corporations. What follows is a gripping account of survival and innovation: lunar mining operations extracting water ice from permanently shadowed craters, political battles over resource rights, and the psychological transformation of people who begin calling the Moon home.

Each book tackles a different phase: initial settlement, industrial expansion, the emergence of a distinct lunar culture, and the Moon's declaration of itself as sovereign territory — the political act that sets the stage for everything on Mars.

Written with deep research into lunar geology, NASA's Artemis program, and space resource economics, **Lunar Foundation** respects both the science and the reader's intelligence. In a market flooded with AI-generated content, this is human-authored hard sci-fi that proves the difference.

**All four books are complete and available now.** Read them as a prequel to No Blue Sky — or as a standalone saga of humanity's first off-world civilization.
