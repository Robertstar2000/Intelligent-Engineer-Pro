## 4. CHANGE MANAGEMENT PLAN

### 4.1 Communication Plan

#### Before Launch (2-4 Weeks Prior)

| Message Topic | Key Points | Channel | Audience | Owner | Due |
|--------------|------------|---------|----------|-------|-----|
| **Announcement** | • What's changing and why<br>• Timeline overview<br>• Benefits for the business and team<br>• Who to contact with questions | All-hands meeting + Email | All stakeholders | Owner | Week 2 |
| **Addressing Fear** | • Proactive statement on job security<br>• "AI eliminates tasks, not jobs"<br>• Focus on growth and new opportunities | Email + 1:1 conversations | All team members | Owner | Week 2 |
| **Champion Introduction** | • Introduce AI Implementation Lead<br>• Introduce Champions Network<br>• Describe their role | Team meeting | All team members | AI Lead | Week 3 |

#### During Implementation

| Frequency | Content | Channel | Owner |
|-----------|---------|---------|-------|
| Weekly | Progress update, wins, issues, next steps | Email or team standup | AI Lead |
| As needed | Issue resolution updates | Direct communication | AI Lead |
| Weekly (start) | Champion sync — gather feedback, address concerns | Brief meeting | AI Lead + Champions |
| Ad hoc | Celebrate early wins publicly | Team channel | Owner |

#### Post-Launch

| Frequency | Content | Channel | Owner |
|-----------|---------|---------|-------|
| Monthly | Usage metrics, satisfaction check, optimization ideas | Team meeting | AI Lead |
| Quarterly | ROI review, strategy update, next phase planning | Full review meeting | Owner + AI Lead |
| Ongoing | Advanced tips, best practices, success stories | Team channel | Champions |

### 4.2 Resistance Management

| Type of Resistance | Strategy | Owner |
|--------------------|----------|-------|
| **Fear of job loss** | Directly address: "We're automating tasks, not eliminating roles. Our goal is to free you for higher-value work." Share examples of how roles will evolve. | Owner |
| **Fear of inadequacy** | Pair with champion for 1:1 support. Provide extra hands-on training. Celebrate small wins publicly. Never make anyone feel foolish for asking questions. | AI Lead + Champions |
| **"Old way was faster"** | This is normal for first 2-3 weeks. Show time comparison data. Focus on long-term trend, not day 1 performance. Address specific configuration issues if valid. | AI Lead |
| **Skepticism from past failures** | Acknowledge the past failure explicitly. Differentiate this approach (proper assessment, phased rollout, real training). Start with a guaranteed small win. | Owner |
| **Passive resistance** | One-on-one conversations to understand root cause. Involve resistant individuals in improvement decisions. Sometimes resistance is a sign of a real problem — listen. | Owner + AI Lead |
| **"I just don't like it"** | Dig deeper — this often masks other fears. Find an aspect of the tool they can appreciate. Be patient but set expectations about adoption. | AI Lead + Owner |

## 7. RISK MITIGATION PLAN

| Risk | Trigger | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Contingency | Owner |
|------|---------|-----------|--------|------------|-------------|-------|
| **Low Adoption** | <50% active users after 4 weeks | Med | High | • Additional 1:1 training<br>• Champion intervention<br>• Simplify workflows<br>• Address specific fears | Re-evaluate tool fit; consider alternative | AI Lead |
| **AI Accuracy Issues** | Error rate >10% after tuning | Low-Med | High | • Verify data quality<br>• Adjust AI settings<br>• Work with vendor<br>• Provide training examples | Add human review step for critical outputs | AI Lead |
| **Integration Failures** | Data not syncing after 48 hours | Low | High | • Check API status<br>• Verify credentials<br>• Test in sandbox<br>• Contact vendor | Manual workaround temporarily; evaluate alternative integration method | AI Lead + Vendor |
| **ROI Below Projection** | <50% of target at 90 days | Med | Medium | • Re-verify baseline data<br>• Identify specific shortfalls<br>• Optimize current implementation | Adjust projections; document learnings for next phase | Owner + AI Lead |
| **Team Resistance Escalation** | Public pushback, refusal to use | Low | High | • One-on-one conversations<br>• Address root fears<br>• Pair with champions<br>• Be patient — some need more time | Consider role adjustments if persistent | Owner |
| **Vendor Issues** | Support unresponsive, tool unstable | Low | Medium | • Document issues with evidence<br>• Escalate within vendor<br>• Explore alternatives | Begin transition to alternative tool | AI Lead |
| **Data Security Incident** | Breach, unauthorized access | Low | Critical | • Ensure proper permissions<br>• Regular security audits<br>• Employee training on data handling | Incident response plan; notify affected parties; regulatory reporting | Owner + AI Lead |
| **Scope Creep** | Client adds requirements mid-implementation | Med | Medium | • Define clear scope in engagement letter<br>• Document out-of-scope requests<br>• Address in next phase planning | Create change order process for scope additions | Both |
