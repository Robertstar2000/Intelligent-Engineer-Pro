## Amazon KDP A10 Algorithm (2026)

### Ranking Signal Changes
| Factor | A9 (Old) | A10 (2026) |
|--------|----------|------------|
| Primary signal | Keywords + metadata | Sales velocity (40%) + conversion (30%) |
| External traffic | Minimal | ~3x ranking weight vs internal |
| Title optimization | Keyword stuffing OK | NLP penalizes unnatural titles |
| Dwell time | Not tracked | Reader engagement = ranking signal |

### AI Disclosure Enforcement
- Expanded to ALL KDP categories (April 2026)
- Automated AI detection at submission
- 3-books/day upload cap remains
- Disclosure does NOT appear to affect sales/rankings
- Non-disclosure risks account suspension

### Highest-Leverage KDP Investment
Build targeted email lists before each book launch. An email to 500 engaged subscribers generating 50-80 launch-day purchases = ranking signal equivalent to ~150-240 internal ad sales (3x multiplier).
