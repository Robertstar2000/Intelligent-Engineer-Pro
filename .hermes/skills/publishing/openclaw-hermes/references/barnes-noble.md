# Barnes and Noble Press Checklist

## Account

- Create Barnes and Noble Press account.
- Become a vendor if selling through the platform.
- Complete contact, publisher, tax, and payment information.
- Confirm country eligibility.

## eBook Files

- Use EPUB for NOOK eBook.
- Check reflowable layout, table of contents, links, images, and samples.
- Convert highly formatted books to EPUB before upload when needed.

## Print Files

- Interior PDF ready.
- Cover PDF ready.
- Trim size selected.
- Binding selected.
- Interior color selected.
- Page count checked.
- Bleed, margins, fonts, and barcode checked.

## ISBN

- Use a unique ISBN for the B and N Press edition when required.
- Do not reuse a KDP free ISBN.
- Make sure the ISBN inside print files matches the title setup.
- Use separate ISBNs for eBook, paperback, and hardcover.

## Content Compliance

Flag or reject:

- public domain titles if platform rules prohibit them
- books where the user does not own text or image rights
- obscene material
- libelous material
- infringing material
- policy-violating content

## Publish and Verify

- Submit project.
- Wait for processing.
- Do not modify while pending.
- Verify product page, title, cover, author, price, sample, and print format.
- Save Barnes and Noble URL.
