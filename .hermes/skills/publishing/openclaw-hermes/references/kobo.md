# Kobo Writing Life Checklist

## Account

- Create Kobo Writing Life account.
- Add payment and tax details.
- Confirm publisher name.
- Decide direct Kobo vs aggregator.

## Files

- Upload clean EPUB.
- Validate table of contents, chapter navigation, images, links, and formatting.
- Use licensed embedded fonts only when needed.

## Metadata

- Title and subtitle match cover text.
- Author name is consistent.
- One author or contributor per field.
- Series name and volume number are entered in the proper fields.
- Description is clean and accurate.
- No links, contact info, or social handles in metadata or cover.
- No keyword stuffing or extra taglines in title fields.

## ISBN

Kobo can publish without an ISBN and can assign its own identifier. Recommend a publisher-owned ISBN for broader partner distribution and professional metadata control.

## Content Compliance

Flag or reject:

- low-quality or unreadable content
- garbled formatting
- misleading metadata
- automated low-value content
- private label rights content
- duplicate content under different names
- companion summaries or workbooks that lack original value
- fanfiction
- public domain content issues
- content widely available for free without value added
- keyword-stuffed content
- advertising-heavy content
- hateful content
- threats or weapons instruction
- private/confidential information
- impersonation
- copyright infringement

## Adult Content

Correctly categorize erotica. Avoid explicit content in cover or description. Check territory-specific restrictions.

## Live Verification

- Kobo page live.
- Cover correct.
- Metadata correct.
- Author and series links correct.
- Price and territories correct.
- Save Kobo URL.
