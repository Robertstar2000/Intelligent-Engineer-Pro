# Google Play Books Checklist

## Account

- Create Google Play Books Partner Center account.
- Confirm age and contract eligibility.
- Set up payment profile.
- Add sales territories.
- Add tax and bank information.
- Confirm supported country access.

## Files

- Upload EPUB and/or PDF as appropriate.
- Upload cover.
- Check preview, images, formatting, table of contents, and links.

## Setup Flow

- Create account.
- Set up payment profile.
- Add sales territories.
- Enter book details.
- Upload content.
- Provide prices.
- Configure settings.
- Publish.
- Confirm bank and tax information.

## Metadata

- Accurate title, subtitle, author, publisher, language, description, categories, ISBN, and territories.
- No misleading metadata.
- No keyword-stuffed title or subtitle.
- No unauthorized claims.

## Preview, Copy, Print, DRM

- Choose preview percentage.
- Choose copy/paste percentage.
- Choose printing setting.
- Choose DRM setting.
- For books meant to be written on, cut, colored, or printed, disable DRM when required by platform policy.

## Pricing

- Set price by territory/currency.
- Use scheduled price changes for promotions when useful.
- Check minimum price and restrictions for adult or suggestive content.

## Content Compliance

- User has rights.
- Book is a book, not an unsupported periodical.
- No illegal, infringing, deceptive, abusive, or policy-violating sexual content.
- Preview settings comply with policy.

## Publish and Verify

- Publish to Google Play and Google Books.
- Check Google Play link, Google Books preview, price, territories, and availability.
- Save Google Play URL.
